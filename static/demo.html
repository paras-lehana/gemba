Five Gemini agents walk the plant. One plan. A named person decides.

Gemba (現場, “the real place”) is an AI operations floor for small and mid-size manufacturers. It turns scattered alarms into one plan a plant can test, check and act on today: a worn bearing, the scrap it causes, the order it puts at risk and the energy it wastes.

Open the live plant → ☁ Run it on Google Cloud Run Source code on GitHub Watch on YouTube

No sign-in needed. Every visitor gets a private copy of the simulated plant, so you can approve plans and run drills freely.

One problem, four departments, one walk

The five agents look at the same plant at the same time, each through one word of the theme. Gemba’s Lead links what they find, like falling dominoes, and writes plan cards whose impact the plant simulator computes, not the language model.

  1. ReliabilityA spindle bearing is wearingVMC-204 vibration rises steadily; Gemba gives a time-to-trip range, not just a red light.
  2. QualityScrap climbs to 3.4 %Against the machine’s normal 0.8 %. Gemini vision boxes the chatter marks on the part photo.
  3. SupplyAn order slips to 97 % coverFewer good parts put customer order SO-24117 at risk; the spare bearing is in another plant.
  4. Energy25 % more energy per good partA struggling machine wastes power and carbon; off-peak tariffs show the cheapest repair window.
  5. FlowThe whole line slows downOEE drops; the plan protects the line, not just the machine.
  6. Gemba Lead · the planMove the spare bearing, repair VMC-204 off-peak, protect the orderSimulated over the next days: order cover back above 100 %, about 32 machine-hours saved. Nothing happens until a named person approves.

Why plants can trust it

Checked before it is approved

Every number on a plan card comes from the plant simulator. Drag a repair to another time and see good parts, scrap and money change before anyone signs.

A person always decides

Agents propose; a named person approves; work orders follow. A frozen sensor never sends a crew, and every agent step is in the audit log.

It teaches while it helps

Learn mode, drills on the live plant and plain-language explanations of every term, so a beginner can run the floor on day one.

Built with Google

  • Gemini powers every agent, the Lead’s plan, vision inspection with boxes, voice help on the shop floor and the Learn-mode tutor.
  • Gemini image generation made the sample part photos the Quality station inspects.
  • Google Cloud Run runs the whole product as one container; on Cloud Run, Gemini is called through Google Cloud’s Agent Platform (Vertex AI) with a service account, no keys in code.
